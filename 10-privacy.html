Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

NETMAX is a free Android discovery and watchlist app. It helps you find movies, series and anime, search any title, and keep a list of what you want to watch. NETMAX does not host, upload or sell any video content.

What we collect

  • Account and sync data, if you create a profile or sign in: your profile name and the watchlist, library and watch progress you save, so they can sync across your devices.
  • Basic app data needed to run the app, such as your settings and preferences, stored on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not show you third-party ads based on your personal data.
  • We do not ask for access to your contacts, messages, microphone or precise location.

Third-party services

NETMAX shows titles, artwork, ratings and cast details from The Movie Database (TMDB). NETMAX uses TMDB but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Account sync is handled by a hosted database service. If you connect an optional tracking provider, that provider's own privacy policy applies to the data you share with it.

Content

NETMAX is a discovery and tracking tool. Anything you open outside the app is provided by third parties, and you are responsible for following the laws and terms of the services you use.

Data storage and deletion

Your watchlist and library are tied to your profile. You can ask us to delete your data by contacting the developer through GitHub (see the Developer page). We will delete it on request.

Children

NETMAX is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Open source

NETMAX is based on the open-source Nuvio project (GPLv3). Source code: github.com/devendrsinghchundawat78-ship-it/netmax-v3.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions: contact the developer on GitHub.
